Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clipper Studio (the "Application"), developed by ClipProvit ("we", "us"), handles information when you connect the Application to third-party platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, and Facebook.

1. Data We Access

When you connect a platform account, you grant the Application access to specific scopes via official OAuth authorization:

  • YouTube (Google): youtube.upload and youtube.readonly — used to upload videos to your channel and read basic channel profile information (channel name) to display your connection status.
  • TikTok: video.upload, video.publish, and user.info.basic — used to upload and publish videos on your behalf and display your display name.
  • Facebook Pages (Meta): pages_show_list, pages_manage_posts, and pages_read_engagement — used to list the Pages you manage and publish videos to a Page you explicitly select.

The Application never reads or modifies data beyond what these scopes allow and only acts when you explicitly request an upload or publish action.

2. Local Storage — No Backend Servers

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on your device, in an encrypted-at-rest location under your user directory (~/.clipper-studio/) with restrictive file permissions. We do not operate any backend server that receives, stores, or processes your credentials, tokens, videos, or transcripts. Your video files are processed locally on your machine.

3. How Your Information Is Used

  • To display which accounts are connected (platform name and basic profile name).
  • To perform uploads or publications that you initiate.
  • To refresh expired connection sessions so you do not need to re-authorize frequently.

We do not: sell your data, use it for advertising, build user profiles, or transfer it to any third party for unrelated purposes.

4. Sharing With Third Parties

Your data is shared only with the platform you choose to publish to, strictly as needed to fulfill your requested action (for example, sending a video file to YouTube's upload API). No other sharing occurs.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Clipper Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your sign-in data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Tokens persist locally until you disconnect the account inside the Application or uninstall it. To revoke access at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, select Clipper Studio, and remove access.
  • TikTok: open TikTok → Settings → Security & permissions → revoke Clipper Studio.
  • Facebook: visit Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites → remove Clipper Studio.
  • To delete all locally stored credentials completely, delete the folder ~/.clipper-studio/ on your computer.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Platform minimum-age requirements apply when connecting accounts.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as features change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
